06BEFORE WE BEGIN

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is the difference between Basic and Booking?+

Basic presents the business, its services and contact information. Booking adds an integrated experience with an external platform for selecting services and times.

Can the design be personalized?+

Yes. We adapt colors, the logo, copy, images and services to the business. The scope of changes is defined when discussing the project.

Does the website work on mobile?+

The design adapts to phones, tablets and computers. We review navigation and readability at different screen sizes.

Can I keep my current booking system?+

We can assess your existing platform. If it offers a compatible integration, we review how to include it; otherwise, an external link may be an option. Compatibility is confirmed before choosing the solution.

What is the development process?+

First we learn about your idea and gather the materials. Then we design and develop the website. Finally, we review it together, complete the necessary checks and prepare for launch.
