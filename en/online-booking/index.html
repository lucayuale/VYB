02 / Scope and transparency

Clear expectations from day one

Our Booking plan does not build a scheduling platform from scratch: it uses a compatible third-party provider. The exact integration is confirmed in the quote. If you already use booking software, we can review a suitable link or integration. Portfolio demos are design examples, not operating booking systems for real businesses.
